02 — The problem

In Uganda, many children with intellectual disabilities are misunderstood, underestimated and left out. Communities see disability. We see ability.

Stigma, discrimination and low awareness keep many young people from quality education, skills training and work — leaving them isolated and dependent on others.

Families worry about the future and struggle to find inclusive spaces. ALL ARE ABLE builds those spaces, and works to change how society sees ability.
