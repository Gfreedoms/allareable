Empower · Include · Thrive

All children are able. Some just need the chance to show it.

ALL ARE ABLE supports children and youth with intellectual disabilities in Uganda to discover their talents, learn practical skills and take their place in an inclusive society.

We help young people with learning disabilities in Uganda.
We help them find what they are good at.
We teach skills for work and life.

Partner with us How we work

“Communities usually focus more on disability than ability.

In Uganda, many children and youth with intellectual disabilities face stigma and are denied quality education, skills training and work. Many feel isolated and dependent.

Families worry about the future and struggle to find inclusive places for their children to grow. We are changing that — one young person, one family and one community at a time.
