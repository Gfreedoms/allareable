Our vision

A world where every child and youth, regardless of ability, is seen, valued, empowered and given equal opportunities to thrive.

Our mission

To empower children and youth with intellectual disabilities through skills, talent development, creative arts and inclusive opportunities that unlock their full potential.

The reality in Uganda

Communities focus on disability. We focus on ability.

Many children and youth with intellectual disabilities in Uganda face discrimination, stigma and few chances to show what they can do. They are misunderstood and underestimated, and are sometimes denied quality education, skills training, work and a supportive community. Many end up feeling isolated and dependent on others.

Families worry about what the future holds for their children as adults, and struggle to find inclusive spaces that nurture their talent and independence. Limited awareness keeps stereotypes alive. We exist to change that — by building skills, celebrating talent and changing how society sees ability.

What we do

Three ways we build ability

Each programme is tailored to the young person — their pace, their interests and their strengths.

01

Skills Development & Vocational Training

Practical, tailored training that builds independence, entrepreneurship and sustainable livelihoods.

  • Tailoring & fashion design
  • Hairdressing & beauty therapy
  • Baking & catering
  • Handcrafts & creative production
  • Digital & computer literacy
  • Gardening & farming
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Social skills
  • Simplified, inclusive STEM
02

Creative Arts & Talent Development

Creativity as a path to confidence, self-expression and empowerment.

  • Art & painting
  • Music, dance & drama
  • Photography & storytelling
  • Media & content creation
  • Talent identification & nurturing
  • Workshops, shows & exhibitions
  • Sports & recreation
03

Inclusion & Advocacy

Working with communities, schools and employers to break stigma and open doors.

  • Inclusive employment with businesses & schools
  • Internships & apprenticeships
  • Community outreach & sensitisation
  • Disability rights information
  • Awareness campaigns & events
  • Inclusive camps, trips & community days
Our goal

Training centres across Uganda, open to every child.

We aim to establish multiple training centres and sustainable, inclusive programmes across Uganda that equip young people with vocational, creative, social and life skills — while raising awareness and advocating for their rights.
