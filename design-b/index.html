Our vision

A world where every child and youth, regardless of ability, is seen, valued, empowered and given equal opportunities to thrive.

Our mission

To empower children and youth with intellectual disabilities through skills, talent, creative arts and inclusive opportunities that unlock their full potential.

Why we exist

Too many people see the disability first.
We see the talent.

In Uganda, many children and youth with intellectual disabilities face stigma, discrimination and limited access to education, skills and work. Many end up isolated and dependent on others.

Their families worry about the future, and struggle to find inclusive spaces where their children can grow.

ALL ARE ABLE is that space — and a voice for changing how society sees ability.
