Change the story

In Uganda, stigma and low awareness hold back children and youth with intellectual disabilities. Communities tend to focus on disability, not ability. Here’s what we see instead.

What people assume
What we know

“They can’t learn.”

Every child has unique talents and creativity. With tailored training, young people learn tailoring, baking, farming, digital skills and more.

“They’ll always depend on others.”

Vocational, social and life skills open the way to work, enterprise and independent living.

“There’s nowhere for them to go.”

Families deserve inclusive spaces that nurture their children. We are building them — and working with schools and employers to open more doors.

Our vision

A world where every child and youth, regardless of ability, is seen, valued, empowered and given equal opportunities to thrive.

Our mission

To empower children and youth with intellectual disabilities through skills, talent development, creative arts and inclusive opportunities that unlock their full potential.

Our goal

Multiple training centres and sustainable inclusive programmes across Uganda, plus awareness and advocacy for the rights of every young person.
